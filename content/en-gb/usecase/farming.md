Seraphin IoT can measure soil moisture and emit alerts when the moisture is too low over a prolonged period on time - but 
this is only half of the use case for the system. The advanced BMS capabilities of the platform make it 
suitable for the integration with the irrigation system controls. Serpahin IoT can indeed be used to remotely
control water valves on and off from the palm of your hand.
